Formula Student: Controls, Suspension & Team Leadership

Engineering and leadership work spanning racecar control, suspension design, and cross-functional team execution.

NTHU Racing · 2020–2024 · Vehicle controls · Mechanical design · Systems integration · Team leadership

Team leadership

Led an 80+ member Formula Student team across eight engineering departments, coordinating vehicle development, system integration, and industry partnerships. The team won first place at Formula Student Taiwan 2024.

Vehicle controls

Developed a yaw-moment-based torque-vectoring controller using IMU yaw rate, steering angle, and wheel-speed signals. Implemented the control loop in C/C++ on the vehicle ECU and validated control logic in MATLAB/Simulink before track testing.

The team recorded P4 in Acceleration and P5 in Skidpad at Formula Student Czech 2023. These are team event results; the controller’s individual performance contribution is not quantified here.

Suspension design and validation

Designed and manufactured a double-wishbone suspension with an integrated anti-roll bar for the TH07 racecar. Used FEA to assess the stiffness-to-mass tradeoff, ADAMS for multibody kinematics, and a Pacejka tire-model workflow for vehicle analysis.

Leadership and partnerships

Built industry partnerships and coordinated sponsorship and material support for vehicle development.
