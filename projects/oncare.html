ONCARE · CARE WORKFLOW SOFTWARE

Connect residents, families, and care teams

A resident kiosk, family app, and staff console keep requests, consent, approval, and status in one connected workflow.

Product discovery · Interface design · Care coordination

The product

I built a resident iPad kiosk, family app, and staff console for family visits and care requests. The workflow keeps resident consent and staff approval explicit, with request status tied to system evidence.

Discovery and validation

Product discovery included conversations with about 30 care facilities across the U.S. and Japan. The end-to-end software demo has run with simulated services; a live care-facility pilot has not been validated.

Connected roles

Resident

Review a visit or care request on the kiosk and give consent.

Family

Make requests and follow their status in the family app.

Care team

Review and approve requests from the staff console.

Robot gateway

OnCare’s robot gateway has been tested with a simulated robot. The full autonomous care-task workflow remains in development.
