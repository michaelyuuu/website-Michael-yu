Autonomous Tower of Hanoi with a 4-DOF Arm

A physical robot solves the puzzle through inverse kinematics and time-parameterized motion.

National Tsing Hua University · 2024 · ROS · Python · First-place project award

The project

The system computes the optimal move sequence, solves a custom inverse-kinematics problem for each pick and place, and sends jerk-limited trajectories to the arm.

How it works

  1. Plan the legal disk moves for the Tower of Hanoi puzzle.
  2. Map pick and place poses to arm joint targets through inverse kinematics.
  3. Execute each move with time-parameterized motion and observe the physical result.
