Humanoid Robots for Medicine: dVRK Tool Integration

Exploring the mechanical and control interface between a humanoid arm and a surgical instrument.

Advanced Robotics and Controls Lab · UC San Diego · Ongoing research

The project

My work focuses on a mechatronic interface for attaching a da Vinci Research Kit instrument to a Unitree humanoid platform, with control mapping and kinematic calibration for the cable-driven tool.

How it works

  1. Prototype the mechanical interface and validate fit through rapid iterations.
  2. Map robot actuation to the surgical tool’s degrees of freedom.
  3. Calibrate the kinematic relationship so motion commands correspond to the instrument response.

How to read this work

The pictured support rig is a reference image from a third party. My research contribution described here concerns the instrument interface and calibration.
