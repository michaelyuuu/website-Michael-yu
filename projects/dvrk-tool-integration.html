My work in the lab

ARCLab, directed by Prof. Michael Yip at UC San Diego, works across surgical robotics, robot design, and robot learning. My work focuses on a mechatronic interface for attaching a da Vinci Research Kit instrument to a Unitree humanoid platform, with control mapping and kinematic calibration for the cable-driven tool.

How it works

  1. Prototype the mechanical interface and validate fit through rapid iterations.
  2. Map robot actuation to the surgical tool’s degrees of freedom.
  3. Calibrate the kinematic relationship so motion commands correspond to the instrument response.
