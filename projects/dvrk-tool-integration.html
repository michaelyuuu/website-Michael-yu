Humanoid Robots for Medicine: dVRK Tool Integration

A research project integrating da Vinci surgical tools with a humanoid robot through custom hardware, control mapping, and kinematic calibration.

Graduate Student Researcher, Advanced Robotics and Controls Lab, UC San Diego · Ongoing · Robot manipulation · Mechatronics · Kinematics · Control

System integration

Prototyped a custom mechatronic interface to connect da Vinci Research Kit (dVRK) surgical instruments with a Unitree humanoid arm. Rapid 3D printed iterations were used to validate mechanical fit and sensor placement.

Control and calibration

Developed low-level mappings between humanoid joint actuators and the multi-degree-of-freedom surgical end effector. Implemented kinematic calibration to address the cable-driven coupling in the tool mechanism.

Engineering focus

  • Mechanical interface and sensor placement
  • Robot kinematics and calibration
  • Low-level control mapping
  • System-level integration across humanoid and surgical robotics
