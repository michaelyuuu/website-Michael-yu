Nonlinearity Modification of CMOS-MEMS Resonators

Use structural changes to tune the nonlinear response of microfabricated resonators.

National Tsing Hua University · Conference publication · 2024

Read paper ↗

The project

This study investigated how geometry and capacitive effects influence resonator nonlinearity and power handling. The work combined device design, fabrication, and measured frequency response.

How it works

  1. Design cantilever and fixed-fixed beam variants with coupling-spring features.
  2. Fabricate the devices in a 0.35 μm CMOS process and release the structures.
  3. Measure the response and compare softening or hardening behavior across designs.
