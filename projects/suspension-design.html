The project

As suspension lead, I worked across geometry, structural analysis, manufacturing, and vehicle integration for the TH07 double-wishbone suspension.

How it works

  1. Define suspension geometry and inboard packaging around vehicle constraints.
  2. Evaluate stiffness and mass with FEA and check kinematics in ADAMS.
  3. Manufacture and assemble the rockers, links, and anti-roll components, then refine the setup through tests.
